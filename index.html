Questions

Before the appointment.

What identification should I bring?

Each person signing needs a photo ID that includes the signer's signature. Call Julie's team before the appointment if you are unsure whether your ID can be accepted.

Should I sign before the appointment?

Do not assume you should sign early. Some notarial acts require the signature to be made in the notary's presence. Ask the receiving agency if you are uncertain.

Can Julie tell me which certificate I need?

No. Julie cannot choose a notarial certificate, prepare legal documents or give legal advice. Ask the document's receiving agency or an attorney.

How much will the appointment cost?

Mobile travel is $40 in the Salt Lake City area, $50 for the airport, Alpine and Bluffdale, and $75 for Park City, Tooele, Orem/Provo and Ogden. Signing is $10 per document. A team-provided witness is $10. After-hours and holiday rates are higher.

How can I pay?

The team accepts cash or Venmo.

Where can we meet?

Can Julie provide a witness?

Discuss witness requirements before the appointment. Confirm who may serve as a witness and whether you or Julie will coordinate that person.

Is the online request a confirmed appointment?

No. Julie will reply directly to confirm availability, preparation, location and price.
