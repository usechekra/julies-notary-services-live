Notarization for existing documents

Property documents can involve acknowledgments, jurats, signature witnessing or other requirements. The document provider, title company, receiving agency or attorney must identify the required act. Julie performs the authorized notarization requested.

Before the appointment

  • Check that every page is present
  • Confirm all required signers
  • Ask whether witnesses are needed
  • Know where the completed document must go

At the appointment

  • Julie verifies identity
  • The signer participates personally
  • The requested act is completed
  • Any next delivery step remains the client's responsibility unless agreed otherwise
Important: A notary does not review the legal effect of a property document or advise whether a signer should complete it.
