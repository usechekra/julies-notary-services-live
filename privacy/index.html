Privacy Policy

Last updated September 25, 2026.

Do not send Social Security numbers, identification numbers, medical details or sensitive document contents through this website.

Information you provide

The appointment request asks for your name, phone number, service category, preferred time, city or ZIP code, meeting type, signer count, witness needs and optional basic notes.

How the request works

The form prepares a text message on your device. You choose whether to send it to Julie's Notary Services. The website does not upload your document or collect payment.

How information is used

Information you send is used to review your request, contact you, arrange the appointment and provide the requested service. Standard phone-carrier messaging and data practices may apply.

Your choices

You may call instead of using the form. Ask Julie directly if you want a prior message deleted when it is no longer needed for business or legal recordkeeping.

Contact
